# VAMPIRe
Handover of code for auto-processing of data to generate the 2021 VAMPIRE and eVAMPIRE indexes for Adelaide,  posted on 27 September 2026.

## Further information about the original and updated VAMPIRE indices will be added to this section

## The data on which the indices are based are not included in this repository. 
Although the data is public it is owned by other organizations (ABS, AAA , AECR). The datasets include:
- [1] Postcodes and Postal Areas, ABS - https://www.abs.gov.au/websitedbs/censushome.nsf/home/factsheetspoa?opendocument&navpos=450
- [2] ABS. 2021. “Number of Motor Vehicles (Ranges) (VEHRD).” Australian Bureau of Statistics, October 15. https://www.abs.gov.au/census/guide-census-data/census-dictionary/2021/variables-topic/transport/number-motor-vehicles-ranges-vehrd.
- [3] ABS. 2021. “Method of Travel to Work (6 Travel Modes) (MTW06P).” Australian Bureau of Statistics, October 15. https://www.abs.gov.au/census/guide-census-data/census-dictionary/2021/variables-topic/transport/method-travel-work-6-travel-modes-mtw06p.
- [4] ABS. 2021. “Tenure Type (TEND).” Australian Bureau of Statistics, October 15. https://www.abs.gov.au/census/guide-census-data/census-dictionary/2021/variables-topic/housing/tenure-type-tend.
- [5] ABS. 2021. “Total Household Income as Stated (Weekly) (HINASD).” Australian Bureau of Statistics, October 15. https://www.abs.gov.au/census/guide-census-data/census-dictionary/2021/variables-topic/income-and-work/total-household-income-stated-weekly-hinasd.
- [6] ABS. 2021. “Tenure and Landlord Type (TENLLD).” Australian Bureau of Statistics. https://www.abs.gov.au/census/guide-census-data/census-dictionary/2021/variables-topic/housing/tenure-and-landlord-type-tenlld.
- [7] AAA. n.d. “Electric Vehicle Index.” Australian Automobile Association. Accessed May 7, 2026. https://www.aaa.asn.au/research-data/electric-vehicle/.
- [8] Australian Clean Energy Regulator. 2026. “Small-Scale Installation Postcode Data.” April 16. https://cer.gov.au/markets/reports-and-data/small-scale-installation-postcode-data.

These datasets should be placed into the /data/poa2021/ folder (empty in repository) and should match the relevant name references in the code.
